## Overview

**Are we still solving the right problems?** Aerial robotics has reached a remarkable level of maturity. Autonomous navigation, agile flight, perception, control, physical interaction, and multi-robot cooperation have all achieved major advances. This success, however, creates a new scientific challenge: as platforms, methodologies, and benchmarks consolidate, research naturally becomes increasingly effective at refining familiar solutions, while potentially overlooking new problem formulations and research paradigms.

**Now is a particularly timely moment for the community to question what should come next.** Recent breakthroughs are already challenging long-standing conventions: generalist policies question platform-specific control; unconventional sensing challenges reliance on standard perception stacks; embodied and morphological intelligence shifts functionality from computation toward the physical system; multimodal designs blur the boundary between flying and terrestrial robots; and advancing physical interaction expands what aerial robots can do.

**Wake Up, Aerial Robotics** will use these developments as starting points to ask: _Which established paradigms should we reconsider? Which important problems are we overlooking? Which emerging ideas could produce new capabilities over the next decade?_ Rather than being organized around traditional subfields, the workshop will bring together perspectives spanning learning and control, sensing, embodiment, physical interaction, multimodal locomotion, multi-robot systems, and real-world deployment.
