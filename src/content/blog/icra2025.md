The **25 Years of Aerial Robotics: Challenges and Opportunities** workshop took place on Friday, May 23, 2025, from 8:30 am to 5:30 pm in Meeting Room 410 in Atlanta. The program reflected on the field’s progress and explored future directions. See the [official ICRA 2025 event page](https://2025.ieee-icra.org/event/25-years-of-aerial-robotics-challenges-and-opportunities/) and the [workshop agenda](https://aerial-robotics-workshop-icra.com/agenda-2025-workshop/).
