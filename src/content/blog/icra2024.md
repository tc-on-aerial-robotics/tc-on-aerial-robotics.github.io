The **Bioinspired, Soft and Other Novel Design Paradigms for Aerial Robotics** workshop took place on May 13, 2024, in Room North G301 at PACIFICO Yokohama. It was a full-day hybrid event supported by the IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Aerial Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles.

The workshop explored bioinspired and soft-robotics approaches to building adaptable, safe, and energy-efficient aerial systems. See the [original workshop website](https://aerial-robotics-workshop-icra2024.com/) and [official ICRA 2024 program](https://2024.ieee-icra.org/program/).
