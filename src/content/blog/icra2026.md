## Content

This workshop will examine how AI paradigms can act as powerful design engines, combining bio-inspired approaches, advanced algorithms, and novel aerial designs to advance the development of aerial robotic systems that are safe, resilient, and trustworthy. A central theme is the dual role of AI: enabling safer perception, control, and decision-making, while also demanding algorithms that are verifiable, robust, and aligned with safety requirements. Building on advances in autonomy, control, perception, hardware design, and materials science, the workshop will showcase how interdisciplinary research can shape the next generation of aerial robotics.

The workshop objectives can be summarized as follows:

- Explore how AI-driven methods can both enable operational safety and embody safety in design, ensuring resilience and trust in aerial robotics.
- Highlight the interplay between algorithms, hardware, and materials in enabling robust and adaptive aerial platforms.
- Identify challenges in applying aerial robotics to safety-critical and dynamic environments, including limitations of current approaches.
- Discuss opportunities created by advances in AI, bio-inspired engineering, and materials science to expand the capabilities of aerial robotics.
- Address the societal, ethical, and regulatory implications of designing and deploying safe AI-driven aerial systems.

To facilitate these discussions, we have assembled a diverse group of experts from academia, industry, and regulatory agencies, spanning complementary perspectives across AI, robotics, design, and policy. The program will feature keynote talks, interactive panels, short presentations designed to spark dialogue and cross-disciplinary collaboration, and one panel and student-led poster sessions to further engage the next generation of researchers in this field.
